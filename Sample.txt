## Background (why you are doing this)

This is the **CIB pipeline** (Jenkins). Its stages include (in order, as seen in the Jenkins UI):
`configuration, checkout, asdf_install, prepare, prepare_dev, version_dev_get, build, sonar, package,
artifact_upload_dev, ...` (and a release counterpart).

The stage `artifact_upload_dev` runs a command like:
`./gradlew -Dgradle.wrapperUser=<user> -Dgradle.wrapperPassword=${CREDS_ARTIFACTORY_SECRET} -DartifactoryUser=<user> -DartifactoryPassword=${CREDS_...} ...`
which publishes the built jar to **CIB Artifactory** (`artifactory.cib.echonet`):
- snapshot/dev repo: `gmiteng-maven-local-dev`
- release repo: `gmiteng-maven-local-release`

**Goal of the change being designed:** right after the existing CIB upload, ALSO publish the same
jar (and pom) to **Dogen Artifactory** (`artifactory-dogen.group.echonet`):
- snapshot/dev → repo `p-6771-maven-SNAPSHOT`
- release → repo `p-6771-maven-RELEASE`

Requirements for the new behavior:
- The Dogen upload must be a **separate step/stage** after the CIB upload.
- If the Dogen upload **fails, the pipeline must NOT fail or stop**; it must continue to the next stage.
- The existing CIB upload must remain unchanged and blocking.

You are NOT implementing this. You are collecting facts so the implementation can be planned.
